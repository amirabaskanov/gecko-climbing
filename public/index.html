Honestly

It started in a Notes app.

Gecko's a side project. We climb 4 days a week, the spreadsheet got out of hand, and every other tracker we tried was either built for runners or hiding the climbing features behind a subscription. So we built this one on evenings and weekends.

01

Free, no asterisk.

There's no Pro tier. No premium features tucked behind a paywall. As long as we keep Gecko alive, it stays free for everyone.

02

Your data, full stop.

Delete your account in Settings and everything goes with you: sessions, photos, follower graph. We don't sell it, share it, or train models on it.

03

No algorithm.

Posts from the climbers you follow, newest first. That's the whole feed. No discover page, no for-you tab, no "climbers you might like."

04

Sends in airplane mode.

Bishop, Hueco, Squamish. The best climbing usually happens where service is a rumor. Gecko logs offline and syncs when you're back in town.

Grade scale

Your grades, your scale.

Gecko logs everything in V grades. If you think in Font grades or your gym runs circuits, switch the scale in Settings and every screen in the app follows.

V5 = 6C+ = Red circuit
Honestly

It started in a Notes app.

We climb four days a week and the spreadsheet got out of hand. Every tracker we tried was either built for runners or hid the climbing behind a subscription. So we built the one we wanted to use.

✓

Free on the App Store

No ads, no subscription. Download it and start logging today.

📶

Works offline

Concrete gym basements and remote crags kill reception. Gecko logs everything on your phone and syncs when you're back online.

🧗

Built by climbers

We made Gecko for our own sessions first, and we still log every session with it.
